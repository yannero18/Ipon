Ipon — Design System Mockup

An authentic, editorial, and offline-first budgeting and debt planner built for Philippine financial patterns. Inspired by traditional paper logbooks, Ipon implements structural calm using a Rice Paper surface textured with asymmetric "organic squircle" corners, beautiful high-contrast typography, and a centered quick-action entry button.
